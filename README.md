### `// About`

Hey, I’m **Emil**.

I wrote my first lines of code at **11**, starting with Lua to build games on Roblox. By **15**, I moved into C and C++ to write low-level software, and at **17**, I branched into full-stack web development with JavaScript, Ruby, Python, and React. Lately, I’ve been diving deep into **cybersecurity, OS internals, and reverse engineering**, and I’m hooked.

Outside of code, I’ve been hitting the gym daily since 2025. I’ve worked plenty of grounded starter jobs (supermarkets, construction, and similar gigs), just putting in the hours. In my free time, I make small indie games in Unity, Roblox Studio, and Unreal Engine, and travel whenever I get the chance. Overall, I’m just a chill guy trying to become a sharper version of myself every year.
